# Duly Qualified brand rules

## Colours

| Token | Hex | Use |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Navy | #14213D | Primary. Backgrounds, headline text on ivory |
| Navy deep | #0B1328 | Behind motion graphics |
| Signature red | #C8372D | Stamps, CTA buttons, ticks, the one key word. Use sparingly |
| Soft red | #FF7B6D | Red text on navy (pure red is too dark there) |
| Ivory | #F2F1EC | Paper, cards, text on navy |
| Ink | #20283B | Body text on ivory |
| Muted | #7A8390 | Labels, small caps |
| Hairline | #D5D9DD | Dividers, placeholder lines |

## Type

- **Hanken Grotesk:** 900 for big headlines and captions, 800 for titles, 700 and 500 for body. Tight letter-spacing on big sizes (about -2 px at 90 px).
- **Newsreader Italic (500/600):** only for 1 to 3 accent words at a time, always in red. This pairing is DQ's signature: a plain grotesk sentence with one red italic word.
- **Small caps labels:** Hanken Grotesk 800, all caps, letter-spacing about 6 px, muted or soft red.
- Both fonts are SIL Open Font License, free for commercial use. The font files and licences are in `remotion/public/fonts/`.

## Logo

- `DulyQualified-Official-DQ-Monogram.png`: the master (navy D and Q, red tail on the Q, on ivory).
- `dq-mark.png`: the same mark with a transparent background, for ivory or white backgrounds.
- `dq-mark-ivory.png`: an all-ivory version, for navy or dark backgrounds.
- `DulyQualified-Original-Monochrome-Monogram.png`: one-colour version.
- Never redraw, stretch, recolour or generate a new logo. Leave space around it of at least half its height.

## Voice

- Plain South African English, short sentences, talking to an owner as an equal.
- The buyer is the owner or operations manager of a security, CCTV or cleaning firm.
- One call to action per video: Comment CHECK (the free 2-minute Tender Readiness Check) or Comment SAMPLE.
- No invented clients, results or numbers. Never mention AI.

## Look

- A clean, warm studio look: navy and ivory, real-world objects from DQ's world (tender documents, registers, stamps, checklists, lead cards, the tender box).
- Motion is physical and confident: things slide in, stamp down, tick and snap shut. No glitch effects, no neon, no stock icons.
