Bayside Valves & Fittings

Established in 1987, Bayside Valves supplies industrial valves, pipe fittings, flanges and couplings to manufacturing plants, municipalities and contractors throughout the Eastern Cape. Our warehouse in Kariega holds stock from leading manufacturers and we deliver to site.

We carry ball valves, gate valves, butterfly valves and check valves in brass, stainless steel and cast iron, with fittings from 15 mm to 600 mm. Our technical team can help you select the right valve for your application, pressure rating and medium.

For pricing and availability please download our full product catalogue and email your requirements to our sales office. Our sales office is open Monday to Friday. Phone 041 555 8800 or email sales@baysidevalves.co.za.
